🏆 Who Plays in the Champions League?

Only the top teams from each country’s league:

In England (Premier League) → usually top 4 qualify

In Spain (La Liga) → usually top 4 qualify

In Italy (Serie A) → usually top 4 qualify

In Germany (Bundesliga) → usually top 4 qualify

etc.


🎯 Beginner-Friendly Definition

The Champions League is a tournament where the best teams from each European country’s league play against each other to find the best club in Europe.


🟠 Who Qualifies for the Europa League?

Teams qualify if they finish in the upper-middle positions of their national league.

Examples (England — Premier League):

Teams that finish 5th or 6th usually qualify

Sometimes the winner of a national cup also qualifies

So the Europa League is for teams that are still strong, but didn’t make the top 4.



✅ When does a team play 38 matches?

A team plays 38 matches when the league has 20 teams.

Why 38?

In a football league:

You play every other team twice

once at home

once away

If there are 20 teams:

You have 19 opponents

You play each opponent 2 times

So:

19 × 2 = 38 matches

That’s why the Premier League season = 38 matches per team.

🏆 Leagues with 20 teams (→ 38 matches)

Premier League (England)

La Liga (Spain)

Serie A (Italy)

Ligue 1 (France, since 2023–24: 18 now, not 20)

Eredivisie (Netherlands — 18 teams → 34 matches)

So the rule depends on number of teams, not on the league name.

📝 General Rule

To calculate how many matches each team plays:

👉 (Number of teams – 1) × 2

Examples:

# Teams	Matches per team	Explanation
20	38	19 opponents × 2 matches
18	34	17 opponents × 2 matches
16	30	15 opponents × 2 matches
12	22	11 opponents × 2 matches
